How to play

Everyone gets a secret character. Everyone can see it, except you.

  1. Make a room Create a room and share the code, the invite link or the QR code. Pick Voice to ask out loud, or No-Voice to type your questions.
  2. Get a character With a Custom Pool, everyone writes one character and nobody gets their own. With a Pre-made deck, they're drawn for you. Unfair character? Report it and the room votes.
  3. Ask On your turn, ask one yes-or-no question about yourself. The others answer Yes, No or Unsure.
  4. Guess Think you know? Make a guess and the others judge it. Stuck? You can give up after the number of questions the Admin chose.

🏆 The fewest questions wins. Use Notes and the Board to keep track of your clues.
